About Yaadein

Yaadein is a wedding guest photo sharing app built for Indian weddings. Guests scan a QR code at the wedding and upload their photos and videos instantly into one shared live album. No app download required. No login required. Works on any smartphone.

How Yaadein Works

The couple, photographer, or wedding organiser creates an event on Yaadein and receives a unique QR code. This QR code is printed and placed at the venue. Wedding guests scan the QR code, their phone camera opens immediately, and they upload photos directly into the shared album. Photos are organized by sub-event: Haldi, Sangeet, Baraat, and Reception. The couple downloads everything in full quality after the wedding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Yaadein works entirely in the browser. Guests scan the QR code and the camera opens immediately. No app download. No Google account. No login of any kind.

What is Yaadein used for?

Yaadein is used to collect photos and videos from wedding guests into one organized shared album. It replaces the chaos of WhatsApp groups and Google Drive links that are commonly used at Indian weddings.

How much does Yaadein cost?

Yaadein pricing is available on request. Contact us for details.

Does Yaadein work at Indian weddings?

Yes. Yaadein is specifically built for Indian weddings. It supports multi-day wedding events with separate albums for Haldi, Sangeet, Mehendi, Baraat, and Reception.

What happens to the photos after the wedding?

Photos are stored for 30 days. The couple can download everything in full quality before the storage expires. Photos are organized by sub-event for easy browsing.

Can photographers use Yaadein?

Yes. Wedding photographers use Yaadein to offer clients a guest photo collection as a premium add-on to their photography package. Photographers get access to all guest uploads alongside their own professional shots.

Is there an alternative to WhatsApp for sharing wedding photos?

Yes. Yaadein is built specifically as an alternative to WhatsApp groups and Google Drive links for collecting wedding photos from guests. It is faster, better organized, and requires no login from guests.

How is Yaadein different from Google Photos shared albums?

Google Photos shared albums require guests to sign in with a Google account. Many guests give up at this step. Yaadein requires no account or login. Guests simply scan a QR code and upload. Photos are also automatically organized by wedding sub-event, unlike Google Photos which puts everything in one folder.
